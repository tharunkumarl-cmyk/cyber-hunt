Mission Directives & Guidelines

How To Play The Hunt

Read each scenario carefully. Your mission is to spot the specific vulnerability or deceptive cyber threat embedded in the simulated workstation screen.

1

Strict Locked Cursor

The reticle cursor never changes into a hand or pointer. No hovering giveaways exist—inspect text and details visually.

2

3 Tries Maximum & +30s Penalty

Wrong clicks trigger haptic vibration and screen shake. On the 3rd wrong click, a +30s penalty is applied and the answer is revealed.

3

1 Tactical Hint Per Task

If stuck, tap the Hint button. You get exactly 1 guidance hint per scenario without penalty.

4

Timer Pauses During Debrief

After finding the threat or completing 3 tries, your timer automatically pauses so you can read the security takeaway stress-free.

5

Anti-Cheat Question Shuffling

Scenarios are randomized uniquely for each participant so no two neighbors have the same challenge order.
